Good to know before you press play

What can I make on the free plan?

Three new tracks a day, from words or photos. Everything you make stays in your sound history.

Who owns the music?

You do. Each track is composed for you alone, and you can use it anywhere.

What happens to my photos and words?

They are used to compose your track and stored with it. Delete the track and they go with it.

Can I listen offline?

Yes. Download any track from your sound history on the phone app.

How long is a track?

As long as you like. Set a timer for a nap, or let it run through the night.

What would quiet sound like today?

Three tracks a day, free. No card needed.
